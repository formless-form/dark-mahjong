# Dark Mahjong privacy policy

*Grevillea Labs · effective 16 November 2026*

Dark Mahjong is a single-player mahjong solitaire game for Android. It is made by Grevillea Labs and is free and open source.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** Dark Mahjong does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information or usage data.

- Dark Mahjong **never goes online itself**. It has no servers, and its own code sends nothing anywhere.
- There are **no ads, analytics, tracking, crash reporting or accounts**.
- Your settings, progress and any game in progress are saved **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- **Tips (Google Play version only):** Settings offers optional tips of a fixed amount, paid through Google Play. Google Play handles the whole payment under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Dark Mahjong never sees your name, account or payment details, and keeps no record of tips. The open-source build has no tips.
- **Updates (Google Play version only):** the app asks the Play Store app on your phone whether a newer version of Dark Mahjong is available, and mentions it on the title screen if so. If you tap that, Google Play downloads and installs the update. The Play Store app does this under Google's terms; Dark Mahjong itself sends nothing. The open-source build never asks.
- Android's own backup (if you have it turned on) may include these settings and progress in your device backup with Google. That is handled by Android under Google's terms, not by Dark Mahjong.

## Permissions

- **Vibration**, for the optional haptic feedback.
- **Google Play version only:** Google's billing library adds the internet, network-state and billing permissions, so the Play Store app can handle tips. Dark Mahjong's own code does not use them.

The open-source build requests vibration only.

## Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
