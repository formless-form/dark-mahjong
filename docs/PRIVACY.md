# Dark Mahjong privacy policy

*Grevillea Labs · effective 26 October 2026*

Dark Mahjong is a single-player mahjong solitaire game for Android. It is made by Grevillea Labs and is free and open source.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** Dark Mahjong does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information or usage data.

- The app has **no internet access**: it does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- There are **no ads, analytics, tracking, crash reporting or accounts**.
- Your settings, progress and any game in progress are saved **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- Android's own backup (if you have it turned on) may include these settings and progress in your device backup with Google. That is handled by Android under Google's terms, not by Dark Mahjong.

## Permissions

The only permission the app uses is **vibration**, for the optional haptic feedback.

## Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
