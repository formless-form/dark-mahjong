A calm, moody mahjong solitaire, played by lamplight: glossy, well-worn tiles on a softly lit table in a dark room, and misty mountains on the title screen. No timers, scores or interruptions.

<b>A darker ambience</b>

<ul>
<li><b>One lamp over the table:</b> warm light in the middle, the room falling away into darkness.</li>
<li><b>Misty title screen:</b> a mountain photograph with fog drifting slowly across it.</li>
<li><b>Dark everywhere:</b> one quiet palette, minimalist menus.</li>
</ul>

<b>A different look</b>

<ul>
<li><b>Seen at an angle:</b> the table in true perspective, every tile face fully visible.</li>
<li><b>Glossy, well-used tiles:</b> a thick glaze with streaky reflections, hairline scratches, aged ivory and engraved artwork; no two tiles alike.</li>
<li><b>Three tabletops:</b> lacquered walnut, green felt mat and a woven Thai straw mat.</li>
</ul>

<b>Relaxed play</b>

<ul>
<li>No timer, no scores, no statistics.</li>
<li>Unlimited hints, undo and reshuffles; every board is guaranteed solvable.</li>
<li>Fireworks and a warm, never-repeating message when you clear a board.</li>
<li>Guided tutorial, auto-save, soft sounds, music and haptics (all optional).</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>

No ads, no tracking, no network access. Everything happens on your device.

Dark Mahjong is a fork of Mahjong Solitaire by PcmciaKai, under the GPL v3.
