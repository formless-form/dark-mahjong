A calm, moody mahjong solitaire, played by lamplight or firelight: glossy, well-worn tiles on a softly lit table in a dark room, with misty mountains on the title screen. No timers, no scores, no interruptions.

A DARKER AMBIENCE
• One lamp over the table: warm light in the middle, the room falling away into darkness. Set its brightness to suit your room.
• Dynamic light and shadow: every tile casts its own shadows from the lights around it.
• Light an optional fireplace beside the table: flickering, shifting firelight, with shadows that move with the flames. An intensity slider runs it from glowing embers to a roaring blaze.
• A misty mountain photograph and a seal-script name seal on the title screen.
• Dark everywhere, with quiet, modern menus.

A DIFFERENT LOOK
• The table seen at a slight angle, in true perspective, with every tile face visible.
• Glossy, well-used tiles: a thick glaze with streaky reflections, hairline scratches, aged ivory and engraved artwork. No two tiles alike.
• Three tabletops: lacquered walnut, green felt and a woven Thai straw mat.
• Pinch to zoom in on the stacks, and tap once to zoom back out.

RELAXED PLAY
• 54 layouts, from small, gentle boards to full 144-tile challenges, and 41 levels that run from easy to hard.
• No timer, no scores, no statistics.
• Unlimited hints, undo and reshuffles, and every board can be cleared.
• A guided tutorial for newcomers.
• Each match ends in a small burst of fireworks where the two tiles meet.
• Clearing a board brings a full fireworks show, with the boom and crackle of real fireworks, and a warm, never-repeating message.
• Soft wooden taps and tile clacks, gentle music and haptics, each optional.

PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking, no accounts and no internet access. Everything stays on your device.

Dark Mahjong is free and open source (GPL v3), built on Mahjong Solitaire by PcmciaKai. Layouts include boards from Mah by ffalt.
